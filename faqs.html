How long does a standard laptop diagnosis and repair take?

Routine diagnostics and minor repairs (such as OS installations, SSD/RAM upgrades, and keyboard replacements) are typically completed within 2 to 24 hours. Advanced motherboard micro-soldering or chip-level diagnostics usually take between 24 to 48 hours depending on parts availability.

Do your UK-used laptops come with a warranty?

Yes. All UK-used computers sold at Wastechnology are stress-tested and certified. Each system includes our standard testing warranty covering hardware integrity, screen functionality, and battery health.

Is my data safe during repairs and upgrades?

We maintain strict privacy and data confidentiality protocols. For storage upgrades or OS re-installations, we offer comprehensive data backup and transfer services to ensure your files and settings remain secure.

Can I upgrade my existing laptop's RAM and storage with you?

Absolutely! Bring your computer to our Glass Plaza workshop for a free compatibility assessment. We stock high-speed DDR3, DDR4, and DDR5 RAM, as well as SATA and NVMe M.2 SSDs.

What are the prerequisites to enroll in the Wastech Academy?

No prior technical background is required for beginner courses. Our instructors guide you from basic electronics and computer architecture all the way up to advanced troubleshooting, circuit diagnosis, and OS deployment.

Where exactly are you located in Bwari?

We are located at Glass Plaza, directly Opposite the JAMB Headquarters in Bwari, Abuja. We are open Mondays through Saturdays.
