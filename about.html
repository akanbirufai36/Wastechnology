About Wastechnology Computers

A journey of technical excellence, continuous innovation, and dedication to customer satisfaction in Bwari, Abuja.

Founding Vision

The Genesis of Wastechnology

Wastechnology was founded by Ahmed Abdulwasiu, whose passion for electronics and computing sparked in his early youth.

He honed his foundational technical skills during his formative years at Hot Cake Computers. His rare diagnostic aptitude and problem-solving mindset quickly stood out to mentors and customers alike, earning him broad respect for his innovation and work ethic.

Growth & Locations

How Wastechnology evolved through dedicated community service and technical expansion.

1. Along Sabon Gari Road

Worked under the direct supervision of Mr. Musa for multiple formative years. Here, valuable customer relations and enterprise management principles were developed alongside Abdulgafar (now CEO of RAMTECH).

2. Beside Bwari Area Council

Gaining comprehensive independence in enterprise management, the CEO moved into a dedicated office near the Bwari Area Council to broaden service capacity and solve pressing computer hardware issues for the community.

3. Opposite First Bank

Served clients for over two successful years opposite First Bank before moving to accommodate larger workshop space and expand training programs.

4. Glass Plaza (Current Headquarters)

Wastechnology Computers Engineering LTD has operated at Glass Plaza for over 4 years. This flagship facility combines a modern retail reception, a fully equipped diagnostic laboratory, and the dedicated Wastech Academy.
